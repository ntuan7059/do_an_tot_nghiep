    \Large{\textbf{ABSTRACT}}\\
\end{center}
\vspace{1cm}

In the context of digital transformation in the education sector, foreign language training centers are increasingly required to improve their management and operational efficiency through the application of information technology. Besides teaching and learning activities, asset management plays a crucial role in ensuring effective utilization of facilities, cost control, and decision-making support. However, in practice, many language centers still rely on manual or semi-automatic methods for asset management, which leads to data inconsistency, limited tracking of asset life cycles, and difficulties in reporting and maintenance management.

This graduation project adopts an information system–based approach to address the asset management problem in a foreign language training center. The selected approach focuses on designing and implementing a centralized asset management system that is suitable for the operational characteristics of educational institutions. The proposed system supports key asset management functions, including asset categorization, assignment of assets to classrooms or departments, tracking asset status and usage history, and user access control. These functions aim to standardize asset management processes and reduce errors caused by manual handling.

The main contribution of this project is the analysis, design, and practical implementation of an asset management system tailored to a foreign language training center. The experimental results demonstrate that the system improves data accuracy, enhances transparency in asset usage, and provides effective support for statistical analysis and reporting. Overall, the proposed solution contributes to improving management efficiency and serves as a practical reference for similar educational organizations seeking to modernize their asset management activities.
